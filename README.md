# LEAD Model Readiness Toolkit
### A Policy Nest Digital Product

**Two Excel tools for ACOs preparing for the CMS Innovation Center's LEAD Model:** a 10-year financial model that shows where shared savings and losses come from, and a five-phase clinical transformation plan with owners, regulatory checkpoints, and risks.

Built for ACO executives, population health and operations leaders, and finance teams deciding how to approach LEAD, especially organizations moving from ACO REACH.

---

## What's inside

### 1. LEAD Financial Model
[`LEAD_Financial_Model-1 (1).xlsx`](LEAD_Financial_Model-1%20(1).xlsx) is a scenario model for performance years 2027 to 2036. Change the inputs and see how the benchmark, risk track, capitation election, and quality score change your 10-year result.

| Tab | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Cover | What the model covers and how to navigate it |
| Inputs & Assumptions | Your ACO's profile and elections. Every input you can change is blue text on yellow fill. |
| Benchmark Build | What your benchmark looks like each year after CMS adjustments |
| Shared Savings & Losses | What you keep or owe under the Global (100%) and Professional (50%) risk tracks |
| Cash Flow (Capitation) | How monthly revenue works under primary care capitation, total care capitation, or FFS |
| Quality Withhold | How much of the 3% withhold you earn back at different quality scores |
| Summary Dashboard | The executive view: 10-year P&L and key sensitivities |
| Sources & Methodology | The CMS source documents and how each formula was derived |

### 2. Clinical Transformation Project Plan
[`LEAD-Model_Clinical-Transformation-Project-Plan 1.xlsx`](LEAD-Model_Clinical-Transformation-Project-Plan%201.xlsx) turns LEAD readiness into a workplan with owners and evidence.

| Tab | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | The five phases, milestones, and the KPIs to track monthly (AWV completion, HCC recapture, readmissions, ED visits, BP and A1c control, 7-day follow-up) |
| Regulatory Checkpoint | Which CMS rule each task satisfies, its citation, due date, owner, and the evidence an auditor would ask for |
| Risk Registry | What could go wrong, how likely it is, and the mitigation |
| Quality Tracker | Quality measure status and gaps |
| Phase 1: Foundation | Data feeds, dashboards, and team build |
| Phase 2: Clinical Activation | Risk stratification, annual wellness visits, HCC coding, quality gaps |
| Phase 3: Optimization | REACH close-out and LEAD benchmark positioning |
| Phase 4: LEAD Launch | Capitation activation, CARA specialist enrollment, beneficiary engagement |
| Phase 5: Steady State | Monthly scorecards, quality improvement cycles, provider coaching |
| RACI Matrix | Who is responsible, accountable, consulted, and informed for each workstream |
| Glossary | Plain definitions of LEAD and Medicare ACO terms |

## Use it in 15 minutes

1. Open the **Financial Model** and go to **Inputs & Assumptions**.
2. Change three inputs to match your ACO: aligned beneficiaries, CMS-HCC risk score, and historical PBPM spending.
3. Pick a risk track (Global or Professional) and a capitation election (None, PCC, or TCC).
4. Open the **Summary Dashboard** to see the 10-year result, then change one assumption at a time to see what moves it most.
5. Open the **Project Plan**, start on **Overview**, and use the **RACI Matrix** to assign owners for each phase.

The sample inputs describe a fictional 6,000-beneficiary ACO. Replace them with your own data before drawing conclusions.
